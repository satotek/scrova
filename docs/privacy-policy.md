# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Scrova records the music you play and sends it to your Last.fm account. This policy explains what
Scrova reads, where that information goes, and how to remove it.

## What Scrova reads

- **What is playing.** Track title, artist, album, album artist, track length, playback position,
  playback state, the player's track identifier, and album artwork. On Mac and iPhone this comes from
  the Music app. On Android it comes from the media controls of apps that are playing audio.
- **Your music library's play history (iPhone only).** Play counts and last-played dates of songs in
  your library, used to find plays that happened while Scrova was closed.
- **Your Last.fm account name and session key**, after you sign in.

On Android, Scrova needs notification access because Android only allows apps with that access to
read other apps' media controls. Scrova does not read the content of your notifications.

## Where it goes

- **Last.fm.** When you are signed in, Scrova sends the tracks you listened to, with the time you
  started them, to Last.fm so they appear in your profile. Last.fm's handling of that data is covered
  by the [Last.fm privacy policy](https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy).
- **Your device.** A history of your scrobbles, the queue of scrobbles waiting to be sent, and small
  copies of album artwork are stored on your device. The session key is stored in the system's secure
  storage (Keychain on Apple devices, a Keystore-protected key on Android).

Scrova has no servers of its own. It does not use analytics, advertising, or crash reporting
services, and it does not share or sell your information to anyone.

## Removing your information

- **Sign out** in Settings to delete the session key from your device. Scrobbles that are waiting
  to be sent stay on your device until you sign in again or remove the app.
- **Remove the app** to delete everything Scrova stored on your device.
- Scrobbles already sent to Last.fm are part of your Last.fm profile; you can delete them on Last.fm.
  You can also revoke Scrova's access in your Last.fm settings under Applications.

## Children

Scrova is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and the date above will change.
